About Me

Finance built around the people who use it

I have spent ten years in FP&A and am a CFA Level 3 candidate in the Private Markets Pathway, sitting the exam in February 2027. At Société Générale, I consolidated €7B in annual costs across 12 business units and managed €400M/year in tax operations for the investment banking division. At Auditoire (TBWA Group), I ran the whole planning function for a €100M revenue, 150-person agency, managed a team of three, and worked directly with the CFO and CEO.

I've always seen FP&A as a product in service of the business: clear, intuitive, built to help teams make better decisions. So I start from what the CFO or the business manager needs, ship a simple version that works, and improve it every month. At Auditoire that meant one source of truth for business data, monthly one-to-ones with every manager, and a P&L per business unit the CEO used to decide where to invest. A finance tool only counts if people open it.

SQL, Python, and Power BI came from the finance work needing them. Closes got faster, forecasts got sharper, and executives started checking their numbers on their phone before meetings. AI agents are the newest part of the toolkit: my pipeline drafts variance commentary, verifies every figure against source, and stops for human sign-off. The goal stays the same: less time producing numbers, more time explaining them.

Based in Montréal since March 2026 with an open work permit through March 2028. French is my native language, and I have full professional proficiency in English. Open to FP&A Manager and Finance Manager roles, on site or remote. If your finance team is scaling faster than its processes, that is exactly the problem I like to work on.
