    How I work

    Planning and reporting that help teams make decisions

    Can we afford a new team? Why did margin slip? I work through those questions with the people who know the business, challenge the assumptions and turn the analysis into a plan. I use Excel, Power BI, SQL and Python where they improve the process, and document the work so the team can maintain it.

    Plans tied to the business

    At Auditoire, I held monthly reviews with business and support-function leaders to challenge performance and update forecasts. The BU P&L I built informed team structure and hiring decisions.

    A team that takes ownership

    I hired and managed three analysts, helping them work more independently and take on broader responsibilities in P&L reporting, treasury and project profitability.

    Reporting the team can trust

    I reviewed monthly P&L and balance sheet packs with the Head of Accounting. I also worked with Accounting and KPMG to redesign SOX controls, reducing monthly control work by 30%.
